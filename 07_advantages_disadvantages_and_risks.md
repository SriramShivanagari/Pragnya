## 1. Comprehensive 14-Point Risk Register (Section 12 Requirement)

Every technical, agronomic, operational, and adoption risk has been evaluated with concrete prevention and fallback mechanisms.

| # | Risk Category | Probability | Potential Impact | Why It Matters | Prevention Strategy | Built-In Fallback Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Incorrect / Overconfident Yield Predictions** | Medium | High | A farmer who trusts a falsely high yield forecast may overspend on inputs or take excessive credit | Use **Conformal Quantile Regression (`P10, P50, P90`)** trained with strict temporal splits (no data leakage) | Always display the **P10 Downside Yield** and a **Confidence Badge**; suppress aggressive input increases when uncertainty width (`P90 - P10`) exceeds 30% of median |
| **2** | **Inaccurate or Stale Soil Information** | High | Medium | SoilGrids (250m) and 3-year Soil Health Cards may not reflect last season's fertilizer additions | Fuse static soil priors with **in-season Sentinel-2 Red-Edge (`NDRE`)** which reflects actual live plant chlorophyll/nitrogen uptake | Provide a 1-tap **"I have a Soil Health Card / Recent Urea Entry"** override slider and widen nutrient confidence bounds when using regional priors |
| **3** | **Cloud Cover Corrupting Optical Satellite Imagery** | High (Kharif) | High | June–October monsoon clouds block Sentinel-2 optical passes for weeks at a time | Filter cloudy pixels via `SCL` band and apply Savitzky-Golay smoothing | Automatically switch to **Sentinel-1 C-band SAR (`VV/VH` & `RVI`)** + agro-meteorological `GDD` progression when optical cloud cover >30% ([MDPI 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/17/8/1412)) |
| **4** | **Small Plot Boundary Contamination (Mixed Pixels)** | Medium | Medium | On narrow <0.5-acre plots, 10m Sentinel pixels along the border may overlap trees, bunds, or neighbor crops | Apply an inward morphological buffer on the plot polygon and take the **median** (not mean) across interior pixels | Fall back to homogeneous same-crop cluster pixels within a 100m radius from the Digital Crop Survey layer |
| **5** | **Regional & Agro-Climatic Domain Shift** | Medium | High | A model calibrated on irrigated Punjab paddy will fail on rainfed Odisha or Telangana paddy | Include historical 5-year district yield prior ($\bar{Y}_d$), agro-climatic zone ID, and irrigation mode as core features | Restrict recommendations outside validated districts to conservative **FAO-56 / ICAR rule-based water & nutrient budgets** with a clear warning banner |
| **6** | **Unavailable or Rate-Limited Third-Party Weather/Satellite APIs** | Medium (during live demo) | Critical (for hackathon demo) | Live API timeouts during a 5-minute judge pitch can break the demonstration | Implement a **Two-Tier Local + Live Cache (`SQLite/DuckDB`)** storing pre-fetched multi-year satellite, soil, and weather trajectories for 15 benchmark districts | If Copernicus or SoilGrids REST API times out (>2.5s), seamlessly serve the pre-cached benchmark trajectory with a `"Cached Snapshot"` indicator |
| **7** | **Lack of Plot-Level Historical Ground Truth Labels** | High | Medium | Public Indian yield datasets ([UPAg](https://upag.gov.in/), [ICRISAT](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ywp3y5j9vv/1)) are district-level aggregates | Train Stage-1 on district yields and use Stage-2 pixel `SAVI` ratio scaling validated against published field-scale literature ([IJISAE](https://ijisae.org/index.php/IJISAE/article/view/2326)) | Explicitly label plot outputs as *"District-Calibrated Plot Estimate"* and collect post-harvest farmer feedback to build true plot labels over time |
| **8** | **Unsafe or Agronomically Invalid Chemical Recommendations** | Low | Critical | Pure black-box ML or LLM hallucinations could suggest toxic fertilizer doses or banned pesticides | **Never use an LLM to invent chemical dosages.** Bound all urea/DAP/irrigation outputs inside hard **ICAR Package of Practices** min/max guardrails | If optimization output exceeds ICAR stage limits, clamp to the ICAR ceiling and recommend consulting the local KVK officer |
| **9** | **Low Internet Connectivity in Rural Fields** | High | Medium | Farmers in remote villages often have intermittent 2G/edge connectivity | Build the frontend as a lightweight **Offline-First Progressive Web App (PWA)** (<500 KB payload) with cached last-fetched plot state | Deliver the final actionable summary via a **15-second WhatsApp Voice Note / SMS alert** requiring near-zero bandwidth |
| **10** | **Farmer Literacy & Adoption Friction** | High | High | Farmers abandon apps that require typing 12 laboratory parameters or reading complex charts | Design **3-Tap Onboarding** (GPS Pin + Crop + Sowing Week) and lead with a single Traffic-Light Action Card + Voice Audio | Partner via **FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations)** where a single computer-literate FPO coordinator monitors 500 member plots |
| **11** | **Perverse Incentives (Free Electricity for Pumping)** | High (in Punjab/Haryana) | Medium | Where farm electricity is free, saving water alone does not save the farmer immediate cash | Pair every irrigation recommendation with **Urea Leaching Savings (₹)** and **Waterlogging Yield Protection (₹)** | Emphasize that over-irrigation washes away costly urea (55–70% N loss per [PIB 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2237709)) and causes root rot/lodging |
| **12** | **Data Privacy & Land Record Sensitivity** | Low-Medium | Medium | Farmers worry that sharing land coordinates or yield data could affect subsidies or tax records | Follow [AgriStack UFSI](https://creuto.com/agristack-api-ufsi-farmer-registry-agritech) **consent-based architecture**; allow anonymous GPS pin exploration without mandatory Aadhaar/ID login | Store zero personally identifiable information (PII) in the hackathon prototype |
| **13** | **Sudden Unforecasted Micro-Cloudbursts / Hailstorms** | Low-Medium | High | 7-day weather forecasts have ~15–25% precipitation timing error during convective storms | Use ensemble precipitation probability (`Prob > 60%`) rather than deterministic rain amounts to trigger urea hold alerts | Switch immediately to post-storm **Salvage & Drainage Advisory mode** if actual rainfall exceeds forecast by >30mm |
| **14** | **Model Performance Drift Across Future Climate Extremes** | Medium | Medium | Record-breaking heatwaves (like March 2026 arriving 74 days early per [MaargX UPSC](https://maargxupsc.com/current-affairs/climate-india-2026-extreme-weather-report/)) push temperatures outside historical training ranges | Combine tree-based ML with monotonic physical stress constraints (heat stress penalty above $35^\circ\text{C}$ for wheat/paddy flowering) | Trigger an **"Out-of-Distribution Extreme Weather Warning"** when temperature/rainfall exceeds the 99th historical percentile |
